# agenthouse engineering 1.9.0

Organization policy can now retain its source identity and report upstream drift
across enrolled repositories without silently adopting policy changes.

- Signed local, mounted and `AGENTHOUSE_HOME` channels; exact origin pins.
- Separate integrity, freshness and permission reporting in policy status,
  session and doctor; JSON inventory across explicitly listed repositories.
- Explicit digest-bound adoption with rule/check/authority differences and
  recoverable transactions. Local changes and conflicting overrides are preserved.
- Required policy-channel evaluation reports withdrawn revisions as failed and
  unknown/untracked authority as incomplete. Frozen evaluation never fetches or adopts.
- Organization skill publishers with scoped keys, licenses, pins, expiry/revocation,
  signed installation/updates and opt-in between-session channels.
- Existing upstream specialist skill and hook ownership remains unchanged.

Read [organization policy channels](../organization-policy.md) for the consumer
migration procedure, bundle contracts, trust boundary and offline limitations.
Updating the global CLI alone does not update an enrolled repository's runtime.
Existing policy copies remain unchanged and report upstream freshness as untracked
until explicitly configured. Git/npm fetching and hosted fleet services are not
part of this release.

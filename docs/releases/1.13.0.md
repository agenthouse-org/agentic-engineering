# agenthouse engineering 1.13.0

This release adds market-validation records and workflows for the work before product requirements: who the customer is, whether the problem exists, and what the evidence supports.

- Adds `market list|new|check` for Markdown records with frontmatter under `.agenthouse/market/<id>/`, or a repository-relative `market.path`. A record holds `opportunity.md`, `evidence.md`, an optional `buyer-signals.md`, and `fit-assessment.md`. `new` never overwrites an existing record.
- Adds four decision-first workflow skills: `ah-define-opportunity`, `ah-record-market-evidence`, `ah-capture-buyer-signal`, and `ah-assess-market-fit`, plus the `ah-market` command skill.
- Checks segment, condition, and evidence references, buyer-signal promotion, dates, and whether a proceed recommendation or product-market-fit claim rests on a met, pre-stated success condition with linked evidence. It warns about late conditions, a met stop condition with proceed, possible personal data, and partially filled decision fields. Exit codes are 0 passed, 1 failed, and 4 incomplete.
- Adds an optional `market` evaluator kind. It is not part of any default profile.
- Routes ICP, segment, interview, buyer-signal, and product-market-fit goals to the new skills; `process where` suggests `market-validation` for those descriptions. The market-validation, feature-request, and product-lifecycle processes and the product manager, market researcher, and sales specialist roles reference the new skills. Baseline version `2026.10.29.1`.
- Records the decisions in [ADR-0024](../adr/0024-market-validation-artifacts.md) and ships a fictional example record under `tests/fixtures/market/example-opportunity/`.

A passing market check shows a record is complete and consistent. It does not show that the evidence is true, and it does not approve investment; a person fills the decision fields. In private integration, `.agenthouse/` is ignored, so market records stay local unless `market.path` points to a committed location. Consumer-owned role and process copies receive the baseline changes only through explicit `check` and `merge`.

## Install and update

```text
npm install --global @agenthouse/engineering@1.13.0 --ignore-scripts
```
